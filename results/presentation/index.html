Overview

Pipeline

  1. 01 data QC as INSPIRE, SVG genes, Laplace-streamline columns, SA index of every column
  2. 02 smoothed PCA disk mean r = 1.5 grid steps, PCA-32, centred per section; radius by the layer-transfer rule
  3. 03 FGW anchors couplings between neighbouring sections (±1, ±2): 1D columns or 2D column × depth nodes
  4. 04 training INSPIRE + FGW loss, 20 000 steps; no-factor variants with Poisson / NB counts; INSPIRE + GAN baseline
  5. 05 encode the adult encoders applied to P0, P32, 3M, 7M (no training on the young data)
  6. 06 metrics mixing, region / area / layer transfer, SA, Leiden NMI split; rank; reconstruction R²
  7. 07 axes spot PCA, column PCA, areal-axis model (pseudobulk), spot-level model
  8. 08 atlas frontal PCA of the segment atlas; our axes on the atlas flat map; Spearman per fPC
  9. 09 development adult axes on the young columns: conservation, variance share, trends with age
  10. 10–12 figures, 3D pages, this deck; 11 = comparison with the previous results

Methods

FGW anchors: aligning the cortex of two sections without labels

Nodes of a section: 1D cortical columns (~200 µm wide, complete L2–L6) or 2D column × depth-bin blocks (8 bins).

Coupling T between the nodes of sections A and B: balanced fused Gromov–Wasserstein

minT (1 − α) Σ MijTij + α Σ (CAik − CBjl)² TijTkl

M: distance of node profiles (smoothed PCA); C: distance along the cortex (1D) or in the column × depth plane (2D, mm); α = 0.7.

Training loss (weight λ = 770): the latent mean of node i in A is pulled to the nodes of B that T assigns to it

L = − Σj Tij/Ti· log softmaxj(cos(zA,i, zB,j)/τ)

Joint mode: T is recomputed from the current latent every 500 steps (after 2 000), so coupling and latent are refined together. No adversarial loss in the FGW models.

Methods

INSPIRE and what was changed

  • Base: INSPIRE LGCN (Zhao et al.): encoder of [log1p CP10k, graph-smoothed copy] → 32-dim latent; decoder reconstructs the features; counts ~ Poisson(library × β·softmax(factors) × eγsection)
  • Added: second decoder for the smoothed features; patch-variance anti-collapse of the factors; FGW anchors (1D / 2D, joint); direct count decoder without factors; negative-binomial counts; all-pairs discriminators; encode-only / warm start
  • Classic mode (chain of S−1 discriminators, original β normalisation) gives weights identical to the original package after training (tests/test_inspire.py); every project mode gives weights identical to the previous benchmark copy

Methods

Smoothed PCA: choice of the radius

Rule: the smallest radius on the plateau of the layer transfer (within 2% of its best value). Chosen r = 1.5 grid steps.

Methods

Areal-axis model

xtrl = fl + θ ct + Σm μm(r) gml + ε

  • xtrl: mean latent of section t, region r, layer l (≥ 5 bins), dimensions scaled to unit SD
  • fl layer profile and θ ct sequencing depth of the section are removed; the residual region × (layer, dim) matrix is decomposed by SVD
  • μm(r): score of region r on areal axis m, shared by all layers and sections; gml: its loading in layer l
  • split-half: |r| of μ between fits on the odd and the even sections

Synthetic test (tests/test_areal.py): three planted region components are recovered with |r| ≥ 0.95; pure noise gives no axis with split-half ≥ 0.8.
